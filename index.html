For Joint-Safe Discovery (3 Fields Only)
  • 1. What + Load: e.g., “Band row (20 lb) × 12” or “Slant-board bodyweight squat × 8”.
  • 2. During Feel: Smooth / Stiff / Pinchy (stop immediately on sharp or pinchy).
  • 3. 24-Hr Check: Green (better/normal) • Yellow (mild) • Red (flare → tag movement).
