Track A • Garage Lifts 2–3x / wk

The Post-Walk Warm Garage Stack

  1. Cue (Leaving): Tap a smart-plug garage heater on when you grab the dog leash.
  2. Warm-up (Built-in): Walk the dog up the hill—heart rate and joints are warm on return.
  3. 2-Min Floor: Walk straight into the garage before going upstairs. Do one warm-up set.
  4. Goldilocks Set: 2–3 core lifts (squat/hinge, push, pull), 2–3 hard sets each (1–3 reps in reserve).
  5. Reward: Log weight × reps on a garage whiteboard. Beat one number next time.
Track B • Everyday Muscle Daily / Hill

Hill & Upstairs Challenge

  • Hill Rucking: Keep a 10–25 lb backpack or weighted vest next to the leash. Turn 2–3 dog walks/week into glute, calf, and core training.
  • Upstairs Micro-Sets: Put a doorway pull-up bar or one kettlebell upstairs—1 sub-max set while coffee brews.
  • Stair Tax: Take stairs two at a time or carry loads deliberately on trips up.
  • Zero-Friction Fallback: Too cold or rushed? 1 hard set of push-ups + split squats upstairs still casts a vote.
